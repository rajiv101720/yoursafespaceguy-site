Hi, I'm Rajiv

I'm an experience curator from Delhi. I build spaces where people can be themselves, and lately, the systems that help people get home safe.

Most of what I do starts the same way: someone needs something, and a bunch of good people decide to show up. Community dinners, art nights, mountain trips, fundraisers, a late-night call from a police station. I just try to be the guy who keeps showing up.

No. 01 · since Dec 2023

My Safe Space

It started with a cardboard sign and a white T-shirt.

I stood in CP and let strangers paint my T-shirt. Then came food walks, workshops, storytelling, art nights, Sunday meetups and mountain trips. Over 700 people have walked in as strangers and walked out with friends.

No. 02 · Oct 2026

Detainee Gharwaapsi Yojna

They detain. We bring you home.

When protesters were detained and dropped far outside Delhi, I turned a basic Google Sheet into a live control room. It tracks police buses, reads WhatsApp updates, and matches volunteer drivers so every person gets a ride home. Built with Meghnad, Satvik and a lot of strangers with cars.
